About Ratnagiri Tourism

Welcome to Ratnagiri, one of Maharashtra's most scenic and culturally rich coastal districts. Located along the western coastline, Ratnagiri offers a unique blend of natural beauty, historical significance, and cultural heritage.

The district is famous for its pristine beaches, lush green landscapes, ancient forts, and temples. Whether you are looking to relax by the serene beaches or explore the rich history of forts like Ratnadurg Fort, Jaigad Fort, and Fort Sindhudurg, Ratnagiri offers something for every type of traveler.

The region is also home to Ganpatipule Temple, one of the most revered pilgrimage spots in Maharashtra. Besides its temples, Ratnagiri is known for its stunning beaches such as Karade Beach, Ganpatipule Beach, and Ratnagiri Beach, where visitors can enjoy activities like water sports, beach walks, and scenic views.

In addition to its natural and historical attractions, Ratnagiri boasts a vibrant local culture with delicious seafood, traditional folk music, and colorful festivals. Visitors will find the local cuisine to be a highlight, with specialties like Malvani food and Kokum sherbet representing the flavors of the region.

Our mission is to provide an unforgettable experience for every traveler, ensuring that you can explore and enjoy the best that Ratnagiri has to offer. Whether you're a nature lover, history enthusiast, or simply looking for a peaceful getaway, Ratnagiri welcomes you with open arms.

We invite you to explore the hidden gems of this beautiful coastal region and create memories that will last a lifetime!

Why Visit Ratnagiri?

  • Explore historical forts and temples with rich cultural significance.
  • Relax and unwind on some of the most beautiful and less crowded beaches in Maharashtra.
  • Experience local Malvani cuisine, including fresh seafood and traditional dishes.
  • Take part in the region's vibrant festivals and cultural events.
  • Enjoy a peaceful, scenic escape from the hustle and bustle of city life.
